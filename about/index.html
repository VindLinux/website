About Vind

What is Vind Linux?

Vind Linux is a small Linux distribution built from the ground up.

It focuses on simplicity, control, transparency, and a minimal userspace.

Goals

Keep the system understandable.

Give users control over their system.

Keep the userspace small and simple.

Build the system transparently from source.
